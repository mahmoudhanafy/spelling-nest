Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

The short version

  • Everything about your children stays on your phone or tablet. We never see their names, their words or their progress.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking, and we don't use any analytics ourselves.
  • Scanned spelling sheets are read on the device and deleted straight away; the photo and its words are never sent anywhere. On Android, the device's text reader (Google's ML Kit) sends Google some basic technical information when you scan, such as the device model and app version, never the photo or the words. Voice recordings you make stay on the device.
  • If you subscribe to Spelling Nest Plus, Apple or Google takes the payment, and RevenueCat tells the app whether your family has a subscription. Neither of them is told anything about your children.
  • You can delete everything at any time: Settings, then Delete all data, or delete the app.

Who we are

Spelling Nest is made by Sara Hassan, trading as Spelling Nest ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the small amount of information described here that leaves your device. If you have a question about your information, email spelling.nest.app@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Spelling Nest is built so that children's information never has to leave the phone or tablet it is used on. The app keeps these things in its own private storage on the device:

  • each child's profile: the name or nickname a grown-up types, their school year, the picture and house colour chosen for them;
  • their progress: the words they are learning, stars, stickers, streaks, the spelling tree, test and SATs practice results, and a short diary of what they practised (kept for about a year);
  • each child's settings, including Learning support settings;
  • word lists a grown-up makes in My words, with any meanings and sentences they add;
  • voice recordings a grown-up makes for those words.

We cannot see any of this, and it is not sent anywhere. It is removed if you use Settings → Delete all data, remove a child, or delete the app. Phones and tablets may include app data in their own device backups (iCloud or Google); that is between you and Apple or Google.

Camera and photos

When a grown-up chooses to scan a school spelling sheet, the app asks for the camera or a photo. The app only uses the camera or your photos when you tap to scan. The text is read on the phone or tablet itself. The app keeps only the spelling words the grown-up chooses to add, and deletes its copy of the photo as soon as it has been read. The photo, and the words read from it, are never uploaded or sent to us, Apple or Google.

  • iPhone and iPad: the text is read by Apple's own text recognition (Apple Vision), which is part of iOS. Nothing about the scan is sent anywhere.
  • Android: the text is read by ML Kit, Google's text recognition, which runs on the device through Google Play services. Its reading model is downloaded by Google Play services when the app is installed. The photo and its words stay on the device, but when a scan is made ML Kit sends Google basic technical information so Google can keep it working: the device's make, model and Android version and build, which machine-learning hardware it has, the app's name and version, a device identifier and an identifier for this installation of the app (Google says these are not meant to identify a person or a device), how fast and how well the text reader worked (for example the image size and format, the size of what went in and came out, which version ran, what happened, such as starting up, downloading the model or reading, and any error codes). Google says it uses this for diagnostics and usage statistics, to keep ML Kit working, fix problems, improve it and spot misuse; that it is encrypted while it is sent; and that ML Kit doesn't pass it to anyone else. Google (Google LLC, United States) handles it under Google's privacy policy. It says nothing about your children, and nothing is sent if you never scan.

Microphone

A grown-up can record their own voice saying a word, its meaning or a sentence. The app only uses the microphone while you are recording. Recordings stay on the device, are not sent to us, and are deleted when the word or list is removed.

Backups you make

In Settings you can save a backup file of your family's progress. You choose where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or an email to yourself). The file holds your children's profiles and progress, so keep it somewhere private. We never receive it unless you choose to send it to us. Voice recordings are not included.

Spelling Nest Plus (subscriptions)

Spelling Nest is free to use, with an optional subscription, Spelling Nest Plus. Buying, renewing and cancelling it happen through Apple's App Store or Google Play, under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

To know whether your family has Plus, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States), a service that checks subscriptions for apps. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random ID the app makes for this device (not your name, and nothing about your children);
  • the store's purchase record for Spelling Nest Plus (which plan, when it started and renews, any free trial);
  • basic technical information needed to run the service, such as the device type, operating system and app version, the store country and currency, and the device's IP address.

RevenueCat does not receive an advertising identifier (IDFA or AAID): the app doesn't use one.

We use this only to give you the subscription you bought (the legal basis is performing our contract with you) and to answer questions about it. RevenueCat processes it for us under a data processing agreement. Because RevenueCat is based in the United States, this information may be handled there, protected by the safeguards UK and EU data protection law requires for international transfers. If you never subscribe and never tap Restore purchases, the app still asks RevenueCat whether this device has a subscription, using only the random ID and technical information above.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to help you, and delete it when it is no longer needed (usually within a year). Please don't send us your children's personal details.

Children

Spelling Nest is made for children aged about 5 to 11, set up and looked after by a parent or carer. Spelling Nest is designed with the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's Code) in mind. It is designed not to collect personal information from children, as children's privacy laws around the world expect, such as the GDPR in the UK and EU and COPPA in the United States:

  • children never need to give us any information, and we collect none from them;
  • there is no chat, no sharing with other people, no location, no adverts and no profiling;
  • things meant for grown-ups (buying, links that leave the app, sharing, printing, editing profiles and settings) sit behind a grown-up check;
  • the most private choice is the default: nothing about a child leaves the device.

How long we keep information

Information on your device stays until you delete it. Google keeps ML Kit's technical information (Android only, see Camera and photos) as its own privacy policy describes; we never receive it. RevenueCat keeps subscription records for as long as is needed to manage the subscription and meet legal and accounting duties. Emails are kept as described above.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Under UK and EU data protection law (the GDPR) you also have the right to object and to complain. Because your children's information is only on your device, you can see, change and delete it yourself in the app. For subscription information held by RevenueCat, or anything else, email us and we will help within one month. We can't see or delete the technical information ML Kit sends Google on Android; Google explains your choices in its privacy policy.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call 0303 123 1113. Outside the UK, you can contact the data protection authority where you live.

Keeping information safe

Information on the device is protected by the device's own security (your passcode and the operating system's app storage). Links that leave the app and every purchase need a grown-up first. Information sent to RevenueCat, and the technical information ML Kit sends Google on Android, is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

If we change what information the app uses, we will update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app before any change that matters.
