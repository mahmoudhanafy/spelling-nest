Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

The short version

  • Everything about your children stays on your phone or tablet. We never see their names, their words or their progress.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Scanned spelling sheets are read on the phone and deleted straight away. Voice recordings you make stay on the phone.
  • If you subscribe to Spelling Nest Plus, Apple or Google takes the payment, and RevenueCat tells the app whether your family has a subscription. Neither of them is told anything about your children.
  • You can delete everything at any time: Settings, then Delete all data, or delete the app.

Who we are

Spelling Nest is made by Sara Hassan, trading as Spelling Nest, in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the small amount of information described here that leaves your device. If you have a question about your information, email spelling.nest.app@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Spelling Nest is built so that children's information never has to leave the phone or tablet it is used on. The app keeps these things in its own private storage on the device:

  • each child's profile: the name or nickname a grown-up types, their school year, the picture and house colour chosen for them;
  • their progress: the words they are learning, stars, stickers, streaks, the spelling tree, test and SATs practice results, and a short diary of what they practised (kept for about a year);
  • each child's settings, including Learning support settings;
  • word lists a grown-up makes in My words, with any meanings and sentences they add;
  • voice recordings a grown-up makes for those words.

We cannot see any of this, and it is not sent anywhere. It is removed if you use Settings → Delete all data, remove a child, or delete the app. Phones may include app data in their own device backups (iCloud or Google); that is between you and Apple or Google.

Camera and photos

When a grown-up chooses to scan a school spelling sheet, the app asks for the camera or a photo. The text is read on the device itself, using Apple's or Google's built-in text recognition. The app keeps only the spelling words the grown-up chooses to add, and deletes its copy of the photo as soon as it has been read. Photos are never uploaded. The app only uses the camera or your photos when you tap to scan.

Microphone

A grown-up can record their own voice saying a word, its meaning or a sentence. The app only uses the microphone while you are recording. Recordings stay on the device, are not sent to us, and are deleted when the word or list is removed.

Backups you make

In Settings you can save a backup file of your family's progress. You choose where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or an email to yourself). The file holds your children's profiles and progress, so keep it somewhere private. We never receive it unless you choose to send it to us. Voice recordings are not included.

Spelling Nest Plus (subscriptions)

Spelling Nest is free to use, with an optional subscription, Spelling Nest Plus. Buying, renewing and cancelling it happen through Apple's App Store or Google Play, under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

To know whether your family has Plus, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States), a service that checks subscriptions for apps. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random ID the app makes for this device (not your name, and nothing about your children);
  • the store's purchase record for Spelling Nest Plus (which plan, when it started and renews, any free trial);
  • basic technical information needed to run the service, such as the device type, operating system and app version, the store country and currency, and the device's IP address.

We use this only to give you the subscription you bought (the legal basis is performing our contract with you) and to answer questions about it. RevenueCat processes it for us under a data processing agreement. Because RevenueCat is based in the United States, this information may be handled there, protected by the safeguards UK law requires for international transfers. If you never subscribe and never tap Restore purchases, the app still asks RevenueCat whether this device has a subscription, using only the random ID and technical information above.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to help you, and delete it when it is no longer needed (usually within a year). Please don't send us your children's personal details.

Children

Spelling Nest is made for children aged about 5 to 11, set up and looked after by a parent or carer. We follow the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's Code):

  • children never need to give us any information, and we collect none from them;
  • there is no chat, no sharing with other people, no location, no adverts and no profiling;
  • things meant for grown-ups (buying, links that leave the app, sharing, printing, editing profiles and settings) sit behind a grown-up check;
  • the most private choice is the default: nothing about a child leaves the device.

How long we keep information

Information on your device stays until you delete it. RevenueCat keeps subscription records for as long as is needed to manage the subscription and meet legal and accounting duties. Emails are kept as described above.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over your personal information, including to see it, correct it, have it deleted, and object to how it is used. Because your children's information is only on your device, you can see, change and delete it yourself in the app. For subscription information held by RevenueCat, or anything else, email us and we will help within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call 0303 123 1113.

Keeping information safe

Information on the device is protected by the phone's own security (your passcode and the operating system's app storage). Links that leave the app and every purchase need a grown-up first. Information sent to RevenueCat is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

If we change what information the app uses, we will update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app before any change that matters.
