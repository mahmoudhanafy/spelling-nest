2. What Spelling Nest is

Spelling Nest helps children practise spelling with weekly word lists, Tricky words (Common Exception Words), games, tests and printable sheets. The word lists follow the spelling appendix of the National Curriculum in England, and the app contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Spelling Nest is not affiliated with the Department for Education or the Standards and Testing Agency, and its SATs practice is not an official test.

The app is a learning aid. We work hard to make it accurate and useful, but we can't promise particular results, and it doesn't replace your child's school.

3. Free and Spelling Nest Plus

Free for good: the first half-term of every school year (Autumn weeks 1 to 6) and the first set of Tricky words, with the games, Test me, Pip check, the spelling tree, stickers, Progress and Learning support.

Spelling Nest Plus adds the rest of the school year, every set of Tricky words, SATs practice, printing and My words. One subscription unlocks Plus for every child profile in the app. To use it on another phone or tablet, tap Restore purchases there while signed in to the same Apple or Google account.
